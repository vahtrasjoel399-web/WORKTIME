# App Store / Google Play — English

## Name
Tööaeg — Shift Time Tracker

## Short description (max 80 chars)
Start and finish a shift with one tap. GPS marks the spot, hours count themselves.

## Full description
Tööaeg is simple work-time tracking for construction crews and on-site workers.

• One big button: start shift, finish shift. A live timer shows how long you’ve worked.
• Location is recorded at only two moments — when a shift starts and when it ends. The app never tracks you in the background or in between.
• Breaks: pause and resume; break time is subtracted from worked hours.
• My hours: monthly total, a per-day breakdown, and every shift’s start and finish.
• Live earnings — by your employer’s hourly rate, or your own estimate (before tax, approximate).
• Works offline: a punch is saved on your phone and synced later. You never lose a shift.
• Estonian, Russian, English and Finnish. Light and dark themes.

Accounts are created by your employer. There is no self sign-up in the app.

## Keywords
time tracking, shift, timesheet, construction, GPS, hours, payroll

## Privacy
Location is used only to mark where a shift begins and ends. Points are kept for 24 months.
